## Contribution Template

Use this template for every entry. A rough entry beats none.

```markdown
### <summary>
- **Type / Author / Date:**
- **What:**
- **Why:** what improves, or what breaks without it.
- **Evidence:** files, commits, PRs, or the incident.
```
